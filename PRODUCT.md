## Product

A one-page portfolio for **Miguel Dario (@dario.motion)**, Senior Motion Designer focused on **sports and live broadcast graphics**, with 8 years of experience. A second page (`/course`) sells his After Effects course, **Motion Pro**.

## Users and jobs

- **Clients** (broadcasters, sports federations, production companies, agencies) *(inferred)*: decide whether to hire him for broadcast packages. They need to see real on-air work fast, understand his role and the client, and reach him.
- **Students** of motion design *(inferred)*: arrive from Instagram; want proof he can teach, then go to the course page and checkout.

## What makes it different

The site behaves like the work it shows: broadcast graphics you can operate (the Paulistão scoreboard you put on air and score goals with, crests, mascots, screens), presented through a CRT / After Effects / TV-control-room language. "This portfolio is interactive, enjoy, take your time and click on all!"

## Evidence (confirmed in content)

- **Paulistão 2026** (FPF · Livemode): the official broadcast package, on air on TNT, HBO, Record and CazéTV. 3 months, 110 assets, 16 clubs, 6-person team.
- **Paris 2024 Olympics** (CazéTV): LED screens for the studios, 24h live; program vignettes (Tamo em Paris, Partiu Paris). Role: Head Project — Motion.
- **Motion Pro**: mentored over 100 students.
- **Pending** (shown as placeholders): 2026 World Cup (CazéTV) and LED Displays Broadcasts.

## Constraints

- **Platform:** web; desktop and phones are both first-class (most visitors come from Instagram on phones *(inferred)*).
- **Language:** portfolio in English; the course page in Portuguese.
- **Contact:** Instagram live; "Book a call" link still missing.
- **Licensing:** the Wildest Metallo Brush font is a DEMO build: buy the license or replace it before launch.
- **Accessibility:** respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere; every interactive piece must work with touch.
